02 · How it works

Four steps. The last one teaches the second.

Select a step to see what it does and what it produced in the simulation so far. The numbers move as the year plays.

We start with every member who has diabetes or high blood pressure.

They are found in Daman claims history. The list refreshes every night, so new diagnoses join automatically and people who leave the plan drop out.

Why a control group?

About {{ s0.holdPct }}% of members get usual care. Comparing them with the members we call is how the programme proves impact, not just activity.

Left out, on purpose

Members who opted out of contact and those in active cancer care. Other teams look after them.

05 · Under the hood
The systems behind each step, for the technical conversation.
01 FIND

Cohort rules compile to nGQL over the Daman claims knowledge graph; members land in COHORT_MEMBER with a stable-hash holdout.

NebulaGraphPostgreSQLClickHouse
02 PRIORITISE

Nightly score R = clinical + comorbidity + kidney + utilisation + age. 13 DoH-mapped triggers produce the plan, alert and worklist priority.

ClickHouseElasticsearchPostgreSQL
03 ENGAGE

Care portal worklist with a consent gate: Member 360 stays blocked until CONSENT_EVENT is logged. Calls placed and recorded in Genesys.

GenesysPostgreSQLClickHouse
04 LEARN

DISPOSITION, ASSERTION and CARE_GAP events are written back; a nightly Spark scan refreshes MEMBER_FEATURE and re-scores everyone.

SparkPostgreSQLClickHouse
Simulation with synthetic members and a fixed seed: the same inputs always give the same result. Each round is a two-week cycle. Rules follow the final risk matrix of 09-11-2026. Not real member data.
